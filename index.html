🛡️ Responsible AI, Privacy & Safety Architecture

Compliance with Domain 1 Guidelines, DPDP Act 2023, and HIPAA Standards

🔒 Data Minimization & Privacy Protection

In accordance with India's DPDP Act 2023 and HIPAA Privacy Norms, pre-arrival hospital alerts contain strictly clinical and logistical parameters:

  • Zero PII Transmitted: Patient name, national ID, age, gender, address, and insurance info are never shared with prospective facilities.
  • Transmitted Payload: Acuity category, NEWS2 deterioration score, driving ETA, and required clinical capabilities (e.g. Cath Lab, Blood Bank).
  • Role-Based Access Control: Paramedics only access the active encounter; hospitals only view pre-arrival packets specifically dispatched to them.

🛡️ Deterministic Clinical Safeguards & HITL

The AI operates purely as an explainable decision-support agent and never makes autonomous clinical interventions:

  • Hard Red-Flag Rules: Deterministic overrides bypass probabilistic uncertainty when acute patterns (e.g., crushing chest pain, FAST stroke symptoms) appear.
  • Adversarial Verifier Agent: Intercepts hallucinations or resource mismatches (e.g. preventing dispatch to a facility with 0 ICU beds).
  • Mandatory Human Validation: Paramedics must accept or override recommendations; all overrides mandate a logged clinical rationale.

⚖️ Algorithmic Fairness & Bias Audits

The multi-agent system is evaluated across diverse synthetic demographic strata to prevent disparity:

  • Equal Acuity Sensitivity: Triage sensitivity is calibrated identically across age brackets and genders to avoid under-triaging atypical presentations (e.g., female myocardial infarction symptoms).
  • Geographic Parity: Proximity routing incorporates real-time congestion models rather than favoring affluent metropolitan hospital zones.

⚠️ Explicit Real-World Limitations

Honest disclosure of constraints required by healthcare research standards:

  • Synthetic Data Origin: All evaluations use synthetic patient models (Synthea baseline). The system has not undergone clinical regulatory clearance (e.g. FDA / CDSCO).
  • Hospital Telemetry Dependency: Re-routing accuracy is contingent on emergency departments maintaining active, low-staleness telemetry feeds.
